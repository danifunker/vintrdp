crates/vintrdp-x11/data/COPYING: rgb.txt and IRIX's colour names
================================================================

Data from X.org (https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/xorg):

- fonts/: X.org's bitmap fonts; fonts/COPYING has their notices.
- rgb-sgi.txt: Silicon Graphics' colour names (sgigray0, sgilightgray,
  sgislateblue...), taken from IRIX 5.3's /usr/lib/X11/rgb.txt (x_eoe 3.3,
  "based on X11R6"), lines 754 to 902, unchanged but for vintrdp's
  header. The file carries no notice of its own beyond X Consortium's
  "$XConsortium: rgb.txt,v 10.41 94/02/20 18:39:36 rws Exp $" line; it is
  X's rgb.txt, under the notice below, with SGI's names added. The names
  are a list of colour values, kept for IRIX programs that ask for them.
- rgb.txt: app/rgb, under this notice:

crates/vintrdp-x11/data/fonts/COPYING: X.org's bitmap fonts
================================================================

X.org's bitmap fonts (https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/xorg/font), as X.org
builds them (PCF, gzip-compressed, cut down to ISO 8859-1 with ucs2any), with
fonts.dir and fonts.alias cut down to what's here (scripts/x11/bundle-fonts.py).
Each package's notice follows. misc/ holds misc-misc, micro-misc, cursor-misc,
schumacher-misc, sony-misc, sun-misc and dec-misc; 75dpi/ and 100dpi/ hold
adobe, adobe-utopia, bh (Lucida), bh-lucidatypewriter and bitstream (Charter);
each 100dpi package's notice is the same as its 75dpi one's.
